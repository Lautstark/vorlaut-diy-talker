# Changelog

**This file is closed, and what it records is the browser half of
`vorlaut-diy-talker` up to the split.**

It was generated by release-please out of commit subjects, and cut one release:
`builder-v0.1.0`. On 2026-08-27 the editor became
[`Lautstark/vorlaut-editor`](https://github.com/Lautstark/vorlaut-editor)
(`adr/0012`) and the `builder-v*` prefix was retired (`adr/0016`), because what
was left here is a page deployed continuously to Pages rather than a thing
anybody releases. Nothing below is re-cut and the published tag stays valid.

Most of the work described below now lives in `vorlaut-editor`, under different
commit ids — its history is a filtered copy, so the ids here are the ones that
go on resolving and the ones to cite. The firmware's releases are `v*` and are
not in this file; `.github/workflows/release.yml` writes those notes by hand.

## 0.1.0 (2026-08-25)


### ⚠ BREAKING CHANGES

* **editor:** ask what a press does first, and offer three kinds
* **case:** every outer dimension moves, so parts printed from the previous model do not fit parts printed from this one. Print all three again, not one.
* a Sammlung holds at most five sets, and every one of them goes onto the device. Layouts with more sets than that, or with ext_vorlaut_active on a board, are no longer read as they were.
